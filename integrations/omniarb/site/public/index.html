price

Candles from Birdeye. Pick a chain — the same contract address trades independently on each one, so they do not have to agree.

markets, per chain

Every contract address has two Uniswap-v4 pools per chain: one carrying omnichain's hook, and one with no hook at all. Their own router only reaches the hooked pool, so the hookless side is invisible in their UI — and it is frequently where the price differs.

supply reconciliation

The bridge burns on one chain and mints on another, so a single chain's supply says nothing on its own — watch one explorer and the token looks like it is inflating. The invariant that does hold is below.

bridges that never landed

Burned on the source chain, never minted on the destination. These balances exist on neither side, so no explorer shows them — but they are somebody's tokens.
